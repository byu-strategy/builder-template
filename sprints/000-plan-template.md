# Sprint N Plan

*Written at the start of the sprint. `/sprint-plan` fills this in for you. Commit and push it,
then paste its GitHub link into the Plan assignment on Canvas by Wednesday, 11:59 PM.*

**Goal:** What will be true in two weeks that is not true now?

**Why this:** Why is this the right next thing?

**Done looks like:** How will you know the goal was met? Be specific enough that someone else
could check.

**Predicted difficulty:** 1 to 5.

**Changes:** Only if the plan changes during the sprint: the date, what changed, and why.
